## Overview

Final-year thesis direction: **thermal transport** in **monolayer gallium nitride (GaN) nanoribbons**, including effects of **doping** and **vacancy defects**, using computational methods (equilibrium molecular dynamics / Stillinger–Weber potential, as described in the publication record).

## Focus

- Heat transport in nanoscale GaN structures
