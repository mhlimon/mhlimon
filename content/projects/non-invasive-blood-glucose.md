## Overview

Undergraduate project (Biomedical Instrumentation Lab, under supervision noted on LinkedIn):

**Non-invasive Blood Glucose Estimation Using Multi-sensor Based Portable and Wearable System**

Explored whether multiple physiological signals could support **non-invasive** glucose *estimation* using sensing + computation.

## Engineering elements

- Photoplethysmography (PPG)  
- Galvanic skin response (GSR)  
- Temperature sensing  
- Multisensor data handling  
- CNN-based estimation approach
